> Use Remotion when… Multi-template, multi-brand, design-heavy work… Anything needing React components or design system reuse… You want hot-reload preview in Remotion Studio

> …any genre where text has to be readable, accurate, and consistent… is a genre where AI-rendered in-frame text is unacceptable and a moviepy overlay step is the natural fix.

## Relevance
